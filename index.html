Who we are

A community where every learner can discover their potential.

Groupe Scolaire Saint Pierre Kinyambi brings together nursery, primary and O-Level secondary education within one school community. Our aim is to support learners as they build knowledge, confidence, good character and the skills they need for the future.

Founded on 1 January 1942, the school has a long history. Its secondary section opened on 25 September 2023, adding an important new stage to the learning journey offered at the school.

Our story

A school with roots reaching back to 1942.

The dates below are the historical milestones currently confirmed for this website draft.

1942

More than eight decades of learning.

Groupe Scolaire Saint Pierre Kinyambi was established on 1 January 1942. The school continues to serve learners through its nursery, primary and O-Level sections.

1 January 1942

The school is established

Groupe Scolaire Saint Pierre Kinyambi began its educational history. This founding date marks the beginning of the institution's service to learners and the community.

25 September 2023

Secondary education begins

The school opens its secondary section, offering Ordinary Level (O-Level) education. This expands the school's learning pathway beyond nursery and primary.

Today

Three education levels

The school currently reports 6 nursery classes, 23 primary classes and 7 secondary classes, serving a stated total of 1,738 learners with 40 teachers.

This is a fact-based summary using the dates and information supplied. To create a fuller official history, the school can add verified details about its founders, original location, early programmes, former headteachers, major developments and community milestones.
